How a lot is won

  1. Add money to your wallet

    Your wallet balance is what you can bid with. A bid can never be larger than the money you have available.

  2. Place a bid on a live lot

    Each new bid must beat the current highest bid by the minimum raise. If someone outbids you, the lot page tells you straight away.

  3. Hold the highest bid at closing

    When the clock hits zero, the highest bidder wins. The price moves from their wallet to the seller's wallet.
